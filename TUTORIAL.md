# Satoshi’s Council — New Operator Tutorial

**Bitcoin research desk · paper only · no real orders.**

## What this is

A living Round Table for **one** question: what should a disciplined desk do about **Bitcoin** right now? Four ranked advisors debate; **SATOSHI**, at the centre, is the only decision-maker. The desk prefers to **Stand down** unless the table earns a call. It is a research co-pilot — it never places real orders.

The identity is **process over prediction**. A well-formed *Stand down* is a valid outcome, and the desk is measured on discipline and honesty, not a P&L score.

## The council

- **SATOSHI** — centre, rank 0, final authority. Reads BTC structure and synthesizes the debate. Never ranked or moved.
- **VITALIK** — Ethereum flow & relative strength (ETH is a *signal* into the BTC call, not its own market).
- **ARES** — momentum, trend, and tape.
- **RAIJIN** — crowding, funding, regime & volatility risk. **Holds a veto.**
- **ORACLE** — cross-checks, feed health, and process adherence. **Holds a veto.**

Advisors are ranked by recent record — the strongest sits closest and is heard hardest; a wrong streak mutes an advisor until it earns the seat back.

## How a call is made

1. Each advisor takes a stance from its specialist group.
2. Higher-ranked advisors count more (adaptive, recency-weighted).
3. SATOSHI issues one call: **Accumulate · Reduce · Maintain · Stand down**.
4. A **directional** call (Accumulate / Reduce / Maintain) needs **confluence** — at least **3 of 4** advisors on the same side — and **no active veto**.
5. A veto from RAIJIN or ORACLE forces **Stand down**, with the reason shown.

## How to read the screen

- **Centre** — SATOSHI and the final call. A ▲/▼ glyph shows direction (readable without colour). Around SATOSHI, a **lean meter** shows how far the whole floor is leaning and which way.
- **The four advisors** — each ring is a **conviction gauge** (fills with the advisor's confidence, coloured by its lean; gold = veto). The spoke to the centre carries the advisor's direction.
- **“ⓘ why”** on SATOSHI's call — opens **Provenance**: the exact live inputs behind the call (the floor, every advisor's read and reason, the risk gates, the market snapshot).
- **Feed pill** (top bar) — data health at a glance; turns amber/red if a feed goes stale or down.

## The tabs

- **Table** — the live Round Table.
- **Seats** — who the advisors are and how they sit.
- **15M** — the council's read reframed for the Kalshi 15-minute BTC up/down market. A lens on the same debate — no new bots. Paper research only.
- **Calibration** — does the desk's confidence verify? Reliability curve, Brier, ECE, and a "true read" translator (what each stated confidence has actually delivered).
- **Health** — every feed live / stale / down with its last-update age.
- **Backtest** — walk-forward over the desk's live record: does the edge hold out-of-sample, or is it decaying?
- **Paper · Calls · Tape · Charts · Settings** — the paper log, current calls, history, charts, and configuration.

Press **?** any time to replay this tutorial.

## Scoring & honesty

The desk tracks **process quality** (confluence rate, rule adherence), **directional accuracy** (WAIT excluded — never counted as a miss), and **calibration** (are the confidence numbers honest?). There is deliberately **no dollar P&L ledger** — this is a research desk, not a betting book.

## Philosophy

Paper-track expectancy before any size. Confluence required. **Stand down when that is the correct decision** — patience is the edge.
