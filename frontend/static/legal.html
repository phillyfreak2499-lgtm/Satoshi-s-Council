What Satoshi's Council is — and isn't

Satoshi's Council is a research, education, and entertainment web app. It shows a simulated "council" of AI agents reasoning about short-term cryptocurrency (e.g., BTC and ETH) price direction, and lets you keep a paper (simulated) scorecard. It is a piece of software you watch and learn from.

It is NOT:

  • Not financial, investment, or trading advice, and not a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold anything.
  • Not a broker, dealer, exchange, money transmitter, or gambling/betting service.
  • Not real money. No funds are ever deposited, wagered, staked, or paid out through this site. Every "position," "stake," "call," and "P&L" shown is simulated ("paper") for education and entertainment.
  • Not affiliated with Kalshi, Binance, Coinbase, or any exchange or market. Market data is used for informational display only.

Markets are risky and unpredictable. Simulated or past performance does not predict future results. Nothing here should be used to make financial decisions. Do your own research and consult a licensed professional. You must be 18 or older to use this site.
