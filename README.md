# Teas Playbook Academy

Interactive football playbook training games for the Teas family.

Built from the Scrimmage1 playbook. Supports **Eagles** and **Carroll High** themes.

## Games
- **Playbook Dash** – Endless runner: collect the correct position assignment for the called play
- **Series Hopper** – Platformer: jump to the correct series color platform
- **Call Master** – Position-specific quiz on real plays
- **Series Match** – Memory match play names to series

## How to play
Open `index.html` in any modern browser (phone or desktop).  
Choose Team Theme (Eagles / Carroll) and your position, then pick a game.

Progress (stars + high score) saves automatically in the browser.

## Themes
- 🦅 **Eagles** – Midnight / Kelly green
- 🐯 **Carroll** – Orange & Black (Carroll High School)

Made for learning the real series, formations, and position jobs from the team playbook.
