# The Council Method

**How to Run Process-Driven Decisions When Noise Is High**

A practical playbook for using ranked specialist input and forced confluence — in markets and in leadership.

---

## Positioning

Most people lose to noise, ego, and lack of structure.  

The Council Method gives you a repeatable way to force better input, rank it, and require agreement before acting.  

It was originally built for multi-agent market research. The same framework improves sales, team, and leadership decisions.

## Full Structure

### Section 1 — The Problem
Why most decision systems fail:
- Constant action bias
- Unranked opinions
- No forced wait state
- Obsession with outcomes instead of process quality

### Section 2 — The Round Table Principle
Specialist roles + independent analysis + ranked synthesis + confluence threshold.  
Why a single voice (or an unranked group) is structurally weaker.

### Section 3 — Defining Your Agents
How to create clear specialist roles in any domain.  
Side-by-side examples:
- Trading / research agents
- Sales / leadership agents (pipeline quality, coaching capacity, capacity risk, customer signal, etc.)

### Section 4 — Confluence Rules
Exact thresholds for acting vs waiting.  
How to handle disagreement.  
Why “Wait” must be a celebrated and protected outcome.

### Section 5 — Ranking and Feedback
How to score agents (or team members / data sources) by recent usefulness instead of status, volume of opinions, or charisma.

### Section 6 — The Decision Log
Simple, repeatable template:
- Date & question
- Agent inputs (ranked)
- Confluence score
- Decision + stated horizon
- Later outcome
- Process grade (did we follow the rules?)

### Section 7 — Weekly Review Cadence
A 30-minute process review:
- What rules were followed
- Where noise or ego won
- Which agents / sources added real value
- What to adjust next week

### Section 8 — Live Tool Implementation
How Satoshi’s Council currently runs these ideas.  
How to read the Round Table, ranks, and debate log.

### Section 9 — 30-Day Personal Implementation Plan
- **Week 1**: Define 4–6 agents for your highest-stakes decisions
- **Week 2**: Run every meaningful decision through the log
- **Week 3**: Add ranking and a hard confluence threshold
- **Week 4**: Full review and refinement

### Section 10 — Transfer to Leadership & Sales
Same framework applied to:
- Hiring decisions
- Coaching priorities
- Pipeline and forecasting decisions
- Team accountability systems

This section deliberately bridges the crypto research tool to professional operating systems.
