## Operating rule

A LAB item belongs in one of four states:

- **ACTIVE DECIDER** — emits a shadow direction/policy under frozen rules and receives prospective grades.
- **ACTIVE MEASUREMENT** — collects/reads evidence but never emits an authoritative direction.
- **MANUAL ANALYSIS** — recomputes from retained evidence on demand; it is not expected to have a timer.
- **RETIRED** — no current research question, no meaningful consumer, or superseded by a better measurement.

No item is retired merely because it is old. Retirement requires proving that its question or data path is redundant.

## What is actually wrong today

1. **The public LAB is narrower than the research system.** `lab-public.ts` currently surfaces exit specimens, seat timing and call-quality. Important active experiments such as v3, TAKER, TAPE2/VEL2/STRIKE2, absorption, path parity and NULL_HORIZON live elsewhere. That makes healthy research look stale because there is no single inventory/freshness view.
2. **Freshness is not normalized across studies.** Some studies expose last-capture health; others expose only counts; manual studies have no timer by design. The LAB needs a per-study contract: type, authority, last evidence time, current N, expected cadence, and whether a missing update is an error.
3. **Decision questions and execution questions can become mixed.** V4 is intentionally direction-only. Entry price is recorded after the side is frozen and may be scored later, but it cannot suppress the call. A future multi-buy/sell profit hunter must be a separate experiment/table.
4. **Do not collapse measurement tools into bots.** Path parity, decision snapshots, higher-timeframe context, absorption and NULL_HORIZON are useful precisely because they do not vote.

## Retirement result

No active research collector should be deleted in this pass. The audit found consumers or a distinct live question for each currently wired collector above. The immediate cleanup target is **visibility and health classification**, not destructive removal.

A later retirement pass should require all three:
1. no unique research question,
2. no current consumer or decision it informs,
3. a superseding dataset with equal-or-better provenance.
