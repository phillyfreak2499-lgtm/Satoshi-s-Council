# Long Desk v1 — research experiment

Status: internal product research. Not public UI. Not a Floor change.

## Product sentence

The Long Desk is a slower Bitcoin ownership-research product that publishes a dated stance on a human clock, its invalidation, and how long the stance has been held, without using the Floor verdict to produce it.

## Constitution

- Shared evidence categories are allowed: structure, tape, derivatives, context.
- Shared conclusions are not.
- The current Chair call, current 15-minute strike, countdown, and live seat votes are prohibited inputs to a Long Desk stance.
- A reader must not be able to derive a Long Desk Brief from Floor votes.
- FLOOR WAIT and LONG DESK STAND are different nouns and must never share a visual component.
- No brokerage CTA, affiliate URL, live-order path, sizing instruction, or performance promise belongs in this experiment.
- Until legal review, this experiment may use HOLD / STAND only. ADD remains gated.
- Every Brief must state what would invalidate its current stance.
- Every Brief must say what evidence did not matter on the long clock.
- The Brief is judged on whether a reader would return for the next one, not on whether a one-week price move made it look smart.

## Four-Brief proof

Before any Long Desk homepage door or public page is built, write four weekly Briefs by hand.

Each must contain:

1. A first-line input that the Floor does not vote on.
2. Observed evidence.
3. What matters on the long clock.
4. What does not matter on the long clock.
5. HOLD or STAND.
6. Stance since-date / age.
7. A named invalidation.
8. An explicit statement that the Floor may be UP, DOWN, or WAIT and does not determine this stance.
9. A source snapshot sufficient to reproduce the factual inputs.
10. A reviewer note: would a Bitcoin owner come back for the next Brief even with no buy link?

## Stop conditions

Stop the product experiment before UI work if any of these happen:

- The Brief cannot be written without checking the current Chair verdict.
- The stance changes mainly because intraday data changed.
- The invalidation has to be invented after seeing the outcome.
- The writing degenerates into generic Bitcoin commentary.
- The only compelling next action is a purchase link.
- HOLD and STAND cannot be explained as distinct states.
