## First line

Bitcoin is still about **33.6% below its trailing-365-day high**, even after a sharp recovery over the last month.

That is a long-horizon input. It is not a Chair vote, a 15-minute strike, or a current-window signal.

## Observed

- **Price:** about $81.3k at the research snapshot.
- **Trailing-year high:** about $122.4k on 2025-10-05.
- **Drawdown from that high:** about -33.6%.
- **7-day change:** about +5.8%.
- **30-day change:** about +17.2%.
- **90-day change:** about +27.1%.
- **Trailing-year context:** despite the recent recovery, price remains well below the prior yearly high.

## What matters on the long clock

The meaningful feature is the combination of two facts that can coexist:

1. Bitcoin has recovered materially from its recent lower range.
2. It remains in a deep drawdown from the trailing-year high.

For an ownership-research desk, that means the recent move is evidence of recovery, but it is not enough by itself to justify treating the longer drawdown as finished.

The Long Desk should resist converting a strong week or month into urgency.

## What does not matter

This draft deliberately does **not** use:

- the current Chair UP / DOWN / WAIT verdict,
- the current Kalshi strike,
- any 15-minute seat vote,
- the current countdown,
- a single intraday candle,
- a one-window win or loss.

> The Floor may be UP, DOWN, or WAIT. That does not determine this stance.

## Stance

**STAND**

The first research stance is no change to ownership behavior. The long-horizon evidence shows a meaningful recovery inside a still-large trailing-year drawdown. That is enough to keep watching, not enough to manufacture urgency.

This is a product-language test, not individualized financial advice and not a recommendation to buy, sell, or size a position.

## What would change the stance

**Candidate invalidation for the experiment:** a completed weekly review that loses the August recovery base around **$69.3k** and no longer preserves the current 30-day / 90-day recovery structure.

This level is a research candidate, not yet a production rule. It must be fixed before the next Brief and must not be moved after seeing the outcome.

A separate upside condition for changing away from STAND also needs to be defined before Brief #002. If we cannot define both directions without looking at the Floor, the Long Desk decision method is not mature enough for public UI.
