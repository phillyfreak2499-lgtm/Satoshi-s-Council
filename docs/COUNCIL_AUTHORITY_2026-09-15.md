# Council authority guard — September 15, 2026

The paper desk sometimes let a SHADOW skill provide a seat's directional read. In the frozen public frame, `CHAIN.oi_with_price` was SHADOW but contributed to the Chair. A reviewed STRIKE card was then unbenched and automatically promoted using the legacy skill score, which measures signal strength rather than a calibrated winning probability.

This change makes card status and existing evidence meaningful at the decision boundary. A seat chooses only an eligible LIVE card for its primary read; other cards still appear as paper reads and remain gradable. The Chair independently turns an ineligible directional read into a forced WAIT before computing score, conflict, quorum and visible rows. That also blocks a stale sticky side from providing a booking quorum when the current read has no eligible LIVE card. A directional LIVE card must have at least 50 graded economic reads, Wilson at least 0.60, average after-fee EV greater than +1.0¢, and its existing manual, walk-forward and per-regime holds satisfied. The invalid legacy Brier is deliberately not used as proof of a probability forecast.

The owner-reviewed labels are applied once when saved learner state loads. Eleven predictive or WAIT labels move from LIVE to SHADOW, two mature negative cards move to BENCH and the four already-benched closed directional cards stay BENCH. WARDEN, ORBIT and WIRE remain non-voting guard/context roles. No counter, grade, pocket, seat weight, threshold, book entry, official settlement or historical result is reset. Automatic SHADOW→LIVE promotion is frozen in both huddle and seat review; owner-reviewed closed BENCH cards do not auto-unbench. Manual candidate acceptance still starts a distinct SHADOW card at zero.

This is a conservative authority change, not a validated return improvement. Paper calls may become much rarer, possibly zero in some regimes. Only future entry-time observations with real quotes, fees and settlements can measure whether fewer calls improve net per fill and loss tails. A late-window correct grade is not evidence that an earlier ask was profitable. The existing 80¢ floor, daily net controls and HOLD-to-settlement policy remain in force. No live venue actions are introduced.

The next stage is prospective probability and entry-time measurement, with an owner-reviewed promotion gate using market and model forecasts on the same eligible decision-time windows. The 82-card review identified explicit `regime_license` fields as missing; this guard enforces existing per-regime holds but does not invent new licenses. The separate non-voting guard-role policy and two-SHADOW-card capacity still need an honest representation in the ladder before automated governance expands.
